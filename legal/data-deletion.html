User Data Deletion

The Judith, by New Angle Studios. Last updated 2 October 2026.

The short version

This app is an internal tool for managing The Judith's own accounts. It does not collect or keep personal data about the public, and it has no user accounts for visitors. Because of that, there is normally nothing stored about you.

If you commented on our Page or Instagram

Your comment lives on Facebook or Instagram, not in our app. You can delete it yourself there at any time. If you would like us to delete or hide a comment you made, or to remove any information the tool may have processed about it, ask us and we will do it.

How to ask

  1. Open https://baritzy.github.io/the-judith-websit/public/ and use the contact form.
  2. Write "Data deletion request" and tell us which comment or account it concerns.
  3. We will confirm once it is done, normally within 30 days.
