Privacy Policy

The Judith, by New Angle Studios. Last updated 2 October 2026.

Who we are

This tool is run by New Angle Studios, the creators of The Judith, an animated comedy series by Eytan Baritzy and Nadav Shlomo Giladi.

What this app is

It is a small internal tool used only by the two of us. We use it to schedule posts on our own Facebook Page and to read and moderate comments on our own Facebook Page and Instagram account. It is not offered to the public and nobody else logs in to it.

What data it handles

The app does not collect, store or share personal data about the public. The only information it receives is what Meta's APIs return about public interactions with our own accounts: comments, reactions and basic insights. We use it only to moderate, to reply, and to see how our posts perform.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell data.
  • We do not share data with third parties.
  • We do not build profiles of people or use the data for advertising.

Deleting your data

If you left a comment on our Page or Instagram account and want any related information removed, see our data deletion page.
